Empowering Kuwait's Industrial Growth with Dedication

Adhuni International General Trading & Contracting W.L.L is a premier firm dedicated to providing world-class solutions in the State of Kuwait.

With a robust foundation built on excellence and trust, we have established ourselves as a preferred partner for Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) projects across the Oil & Gas, Power, and Infrastructure sectors. Our journey is defined by a relentless commitment to quality and a passion for bridging the gap between global innovation and local execution.

ISO Certified Standards.
Expert Engineering Team.
Reliable Supply Chain.
24/7 Technical Support.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional engineering and trading solutions that exceed client expectations while adhering to the highest standards of safety, quality, and environmental responsibility. We aim to be the catalyst for sustainable industrial development in Kuwait.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and sought-after General Trading & Contracting firm in the region, recognized for our technical prowess, integrity, and ability to deliver complex projects on time and within budget.

Our Values

  • Integrity: Transparency in all operations.
  • Excellence: Uncompromising quality in results.
  • Safety: Protecting our people and the environment.
  • Innovation: Implementing global best practices.

Bridging Global Innovation & Local Execution

Adhuni International serves as a vital bridge for international manufacturers and contractors seeking a proficient representative in the Kuwaiti market. We provide the local insight and operational infrastructure necessary to successfully navigate the region's unique requirements.

EPC Turnkey Solutions

From conceptual design to final commissioning, we offer integrated Engineering, Procurement, and Construction services that streamline complex projects.

QHSE Commitment

Our Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment (QHSE) management systems are integrated into every phase of our operations, ensuring zero harm to personnel and minimal impact on the environment.
