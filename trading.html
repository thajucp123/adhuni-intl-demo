Mechanical Products

Specialized Mechanical Procurement

Supply of high-grade industrial piping, valves, flanges, and fittings. We source precision-engineered mechanical components from global manufacturers to meet the stringent requirements of Kuwait’s Oil & Gas and petrochemical facilities.


  • Industrial Piping (Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Alloy).
  • Valves (Gate, Globe, Check, Ball, and Butterfly).
  • Pipe Fittings (Elbows, Tees, Reducers, and Caps).
  • Flanges (Weld Neck, Slip-on, Blind, and Socket Weld).
  • High-Tensile Stud Bolts and Gaskets.
Civil Products

Premium Civil & Structural Supplies

Providing essential construction materials including specialized mortars, waterproofing systems, and structural steel. We ensure all supplied materials comply with local ministry standards and international building codes.


  • Structural Steel (I-Beams, Channels, and Angles).

Electrical Products

  • Cable Management Systems (Trays, Ladders, and Conduits).
